Scatter Plots:-
plt.scatter(x, y, s= , c= , cmap= ' ',
            edgecolor=' ', linewidth= ,
            alpha= );

x = list of x values to be plotted
y = corresponding list of y values
s = size of the scatter recommended=200
c = colour of the dots
cmap = advanced colour options
edgecolor = edge color of dots
linewidth = width of the outline of dots #1 or 2
alpha = transparency of dots # 0.26 to 0.5

to add a color bar

cbar = plt.colorbar()    sets a color bar
                         of the shade of
                         given color

cbar.set_label(' ')
      ↳ sets a label to cbar
           indicating what it refers.
