Feature Engineering
Binning:
Bining is the process of converting continuous numerical data into smaller, discrete categories or "buckets" (bins).
pd.cut (val, bins = , labels = )
This method is used to convert values to binned data.
label = [list of groups]
ex 'high', 'med', 'low'
bins = [boundarie of groups]
ex: [0, 7, 8.5, 10]
'label 1' 'label 2' 'label 3'
Polynomial features:
it creates new columns in our dataframe by squaring cubing or multiplying existing columns together
if degree = 2
1 column x -> 2 and x^2
2 column x, y -> x, y, x^2, y^2, xy
5 cols
helps for spotting hidden data relations.
ex code:
from sklearn.preprocessing import PolynomialFeatures
poly = PolynomialFeatures(degree = 2, include_bias = False)
-> we dont need indicator
new_df = poly.fit_transform(df)
