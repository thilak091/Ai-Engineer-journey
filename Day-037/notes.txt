Naive Bayes:
Naive Bayes is a mathematical/probability wise classifying algorithm that uses prior probability of testing data and conditional probability to give a score and predicts on which has the greater score.
Importing:-
from sklearn.naive_bayes import GaussianNB
gnb = GaussianNB()
parameters:-
var_smoothing
Prior probability:-
The initial probability of the assumption.
Likelihood:-
The conditional probability of any given feature
ex: p ('Dear' / Not Spam)
